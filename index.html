Work Log

Work hours, shifts & salary tracker for Android

Work Log helps employees track their work hours: clock in and out (including with NFC tags), manage shifts, overtime, bonuses and expenses, and calculate salary automatically. Your data is stored locally on your device.

Main features

  • One-tap clock in / clock out, with support for NFC tags
  • Shift, overtime, sick day and vacation tracking
  • Automatic salary calculation per your pay settings
  • Monthly report exported as an Excel (.xls) file
  • Optional backup of your data to your own Google Drive
  • Optional monthly report email, sent from your own Gmail account to your employer — only if you enable it and choose the recipients

How Work Log uses your Google account

Signing in with Google is optional and used only for two features you control:

  • Google Drive backup — the app saves its backup files to your Drive. It can only access files it created itself.
  • Sending your monthly report — with your permission, the app emails your monthly Excel report from your Gmail address to recipients you chose. The app cannot read your inbox or any of your messages.
