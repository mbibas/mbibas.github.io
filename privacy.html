Privacy Policy

Work Log (Android app, package com.bibas.worksclocks) · Last updated: July 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Work Log app ("the app", "we") accesses, how it is used, and the choices you have. Work Log is developed by Matan Bibas ("the developer"). Contact: matanbibas@gmail.com.

1. Information stored by the app

Your work data — clock-in/out times, shifts, wages, bonuses, expenses, notes and settings — is stored locally on your device. We do not operate servers that receive or store this data. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it from the device.

2. Google account sign-in (optional)

You may optionally sign in with your Google account to enable two features. When you sign in, the app receives your basic account identifiers (name, email address) to identify the connected account inside the app. Signing in is never required for the core time-tracking features.

2.1 Google Drive backup (scope: drive.file)

  • Used only to save the app's backup files to your own Google Drive and to restore them on request.
  • This scope only allows access to files the app itself created. The app cannot see or modify any other file in your Drive.

2.2 Sending your monthly report by email (scope: gmail.send)

  • If — and only if — you enable the report-emailing feature, the app sends an email from your Gmail address with your monthly work-hours report (an Excel file generated on your device) to recipients you entered, for example your employer.
  • Emails are sent only when you tap "Send now" or on the monthly schedule you enabled, and only to your chosen recipients.
  • The app cannot read, search or delete your email. It has no access to your inbox, contacts or any existing messages — the permission it requests allows sending only.
  • The report content is generated locally from your own work data and is transmitted directly from your device to Google's Gmail service. The developer never receives, stores or sees your emails or reports.
Limited Use disclosure. Work Log's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

2.3 What we do NOT do with Google user data

  • We do not transfer or sell Google user data to third parties.
  • We do not use Google user data for advertising.
  • We do not allow humans to read your data.
  • Google user data is not stored on any developer server — the app communicates with Google services directly from your device.

2.4 Revoking access

You can disconnect your Google account in the app's settings at any time, and you can revoke the app's access from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

3. Advertising and analytics

The app shows ads and uses analytics to improve stability and features. These services may collect device identifiers and usage information as described in their own policies:

  • Google AdMob and ad-network partners (ad serving)
  • Unity LevelPlay (ad mediation)
  • Google Analytics for Firebase (app usage and crash reporting)

You can opt out of personalized ads in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

4. Data sharing

We do not sell or share your personal data. Emails you choose to send with the report feature go only to the recipients you entered. The only third-party processing is the advertising/analytics services listed above.

5. Data retention and deletion

  • Work data: stored on your device until you delete it, clear the app's data or uninstall the app.
  • Drive backups: stored in your own Google Drive; you can delete them there at any time.
  • To delete data or ask questions about deletion, contact matanbibas@gmail.com.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be published on this page with an updated date above.

8. Contact
